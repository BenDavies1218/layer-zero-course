# 🎮 LayerZero OApp Challenges

Welcome to the LayerZero Challenge Arena! Test your cross-chain skills through various missions that will take you from novice messenger boy to an omnichain master.

Each challenge focuses on one or more of the lessons. Some challenges involve exploiting vulnerable Oapp contracts, while others require building solutions to problems.

---

Use of Claude, GPT, Cursor or other AI to complete the Challenges is absolutely discouraged. You will probably be required to research Solidity or other Smart Contract information, please reference any sources in your solutions.
